Hermes

Hermes is a personal assistant application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

With the owner's consent, Hermes accesses the owner's own Google account data to carry out the owner's requests.

  • Data is used only to assist the owner.
  • Data is not sold and is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Data needed for a request may be processed by service providers the owner has chosen.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account permissions.
